# R4.3 startup tracking and matched motion intervals — candidate

Status: targeted checks pass; full qualification remains incomplete. Offline
numerical fixture only. Historical engines, thresholds, production Safety and
Contract 15A have not been changed.

## Changes and evidence

`StartupTrackingQualifier` establishes an explicitly unresolved observation from
the actual released command and first valid measured speed. It can open a motion
watch without fabricating a resolved command anchor. The first observation time
limits how far back non-response can be inferred. A resolved pair is established
only through the inherited qualified-motion resolution path.

The eight previously missed historical cases S2, S4, S6, S8_stuck, S12, S15, S16
and S16_variant now confirm their tracking faults. S1, S3, S8_normal, both S9
directions, S10 and S14 do not confirm. The unchanged R41-1..8 assertions pass
against this candidate. S5 and S13 verify that qualified motion does not prevent
detection of a later stall. S8_normal is deterministic and prefix-causal.

The initial implementation falsely confirmed healthy S8_normal at 1.6 seconds.
At that point its measured displacement covered 0.2 seconds since the last
qualified checkpoint, while the expected displacement covered the original watch
interval. The correction compares matching intervals. Only qualified measured
progress advances the interval; command updates and sensor outage do not. No
fresh actuator delay is granted at a progress checkpoint, and unresolved watch
age remains unchanged. No threshold or historical input was altered.

## Remaining qualification

The expanded candidate suite now has **42 passing tests**. All R4.2 directional
controls run against R4.3, together with initial sensor loss, startup outage
reversal and invalid measurement controls. The startup outage reversal initially

NaN/infinite speed and release-before-sample timestamps are now invalid observations.

## Follow-up: cancellation, reintroduction and historical replay

The offline startup candidate now distinguishes two cases when a valid sample
shows that the released command has returned to the first observed pump speed
and the pump has not made material measured motion:

- With no confirmed suspicion sample, cancel the unproven demand and its watch.
  A later command starts a new watch and response interval.
- With existing suspicion evidence, suspend the directional demand but preserve
  that evidence and the oldest watch time. A later material command creates a
  new directional response interval; it cannot confirm on the same tick solely
  because the previous command was old. An unresolved watch cannot be converted
  into a directly observed steady baseline.

This is implemented only in the offline candidate. The numeric tracking profile,
frozen historical inputs, production Safety and Contract 15A remain unchanged.
The startup-specific suite now has 46 passing tests, including no-evidence
cancellation, evidence-preserving suspension, reintroduction, and controls for
real measured progress that must *not* be treated as cancellation.

`phase5_r5_2_1r4_3_full_history_evidence.json` is generated by
`python -m v0_2.examples.phase5_r5_2_1r4_3_history_validation` and records
the current candidate and frozen-input hashes. It replays all registered S
sources, all in-domain C sources, and the separate C9R fixture. All 14 adapted
historical invariants, command-domain checks, every replay prefix, and ten-run
repeatability checks pass. Valid C acceptance is **11/11**. Exact S acceptance
is **15/17**: S3 and S14 have qualified measured progress and resolve without
a confirmed fault, but their progress and steady resolution occur on the same
sample. Thus they lack the separate `TRACKING_PROGRESS` state required by the
older sequence contract. This is an observable contract difference, not counted
as a pass.

C9R's A/B/C anchor values remain 0.35/0.51/0.67, but B and C are established
at 1.0/3.8 seconds rather than the frozen R1a 1.2/4.0 seconds. The candidate's
earlier paired-reference resolution is not yet approved as equivalent. Original
C9 is still out of profile domain. P1-P20 have only names/descriptions in the
R4 registration, no executable frozen traces or acceptance function, so no
P1-P20 verdict is claimed. The historical gate therefore remains **incomplete**.

The broad test run also exposes two pre-existing, separate failures: R4.1's
