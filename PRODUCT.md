# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Liquid-cooling project engineers and the Memphis project owner, working at a desktop in an ordinarily lit office. They compare requirements, assumptions, topology and equipment capability during design review.

## Product Purpose

Turn an imported engineering design into understandable checks, a versioned modification and a measurable comparison. Design Advisor is the primary product; bounded research simulation and immutable historical replay support decisions without pretending to certify hardware.

## Brand Personality

Precise, practical, transparent. Chinese-first engineering terminology. State what can be calculated and what remains unknown.

## Anti-references

Avoid audit-document walls, generic metric-card dashboards, decorative pipe animations, simulated telemetry presented as field measurements, and one aggregate green PASS hiding incomplete obligations.

## Design Principles

1. Start with the engineering question and a useful next action.
2. Keep baseline, proposed design, historical replay and new calculation separate.
3. Missing evidence blocks only dependent conclusions.
4. Compare regressions as visibly as improvements.
5. Show sources and precision on demand, not as the main workflow.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard-operable native forms, visible focus, text labels alongside semantic colors, adequate contrast, responsive navigation and horizontally scrollable engineering tables. No decorative motion; honor reduced motion.
